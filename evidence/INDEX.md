# Public evidence index

This repository provides **public claims and their qualification limits only**. The underlying source repositories, commit identifiers, proofs, firmware, raw observations, configurations and research procedures are internal company records and **are not offered to external reviewers**, including under an NDA.

- [Public claims register](CLAIMS_REGISTER.csv) — a bounded description of what is reported and what remains unverified externally.
- [Evidence interpretation](../docs/10_EVIDENCE_STANDARDS.md) — how to interpret the public status labels.
- [External review process](../docs/05_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md) — founder-operated, non-invasive demonstrations and questions; no code, internal data or unrestricted executable access.

Internal provenance is maintained separately by the rights holder. The public register is **not independently reproducible evidence** for undisclosed mechanisms. An interested reviewer may evaluate the public logic, inspect non-sensitive test summaries, ask questions, and observe bounded demonstrations operated exclusively by the rights holder.

No repository URL, internal path, commit pin or proprietary evidence attachment is needed or provided here.
