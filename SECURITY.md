# Responsible contact and disclosure

This repository contains public research summaries only. It is not an operational control system or a public vulnerability-testing target.

To report a potential disclosure of nonpublic information, sensitive experiment details, credentials or IP-sensitive material, contact the STASYS team via [stasysos.eu](https://stasysos.eu/). Please do not publish sensitive proof objects, code, raw experimental traces, access tokens, or target-specific operational details in public issues or pull requests.

No access to restricted source, proofs, internal repositories, raw data or sensitive IP is offered to external parties, including under NDA. Public visibility confers no right to proprietary technology.

See [disclosure boundaries](docs/07_DISCLOSURE_BOUNDARY.md) and [rights and limitations](LEGAL.md).
