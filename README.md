**Lomidze Morphological Algebra (LMA)** is a foundational research program in morphology-native computation. **STASYS** develops separate research and engineering directions based on that foundation.

## Featured opportunity — preserve the mission when conditions deteriorate

**An existing autopilot can remain in place.** A different problem is preserving the *assigned mission* when the operator link disappears, GNSS data become unreliable, or earlier mission options are no longer permissible.

**STASYS Terminal Mission Core (TMC)** is a separate MCU-class mission-conduct component intended to retain an externally assigned goal, take in admitted observations and execution feedback, and form the next admissible **mission intent** from operative morphology and acquired experience. Its potential role is to fill a mission-continuity gap **alongside**, not inside or instead of, a partner's existing onboard systems.

| Already provided by the platform | Proposed TMC responsibility |
|:--|:--|
| Sensors and localization produce observations and confidence estimates | Admit typed context; retain the goal, progress and acquired causal history |
| Autopilot/controller and independent safety authority control physical movement | Form bounded mission intents or justified refusal, **without actuator authority** |
| Executor and independent feedback report actual consequences | Re-evaluate permissible continuations without a host-supplied route/resume selector in the tested scope |

**Industrial scenarios:** geological surveys, pipeline inspection and unattended remote robots. These are potential applications, not confirmed customers or operational deployments. **TMC cannot generate missing position information or override the existing controller's failsafe. Physical arrival at a specified destination without GNSS has not been demonstrated.**

## What makes the technology worth examining

- **Mission persistence:** bounded software experiments retained one fixed goal through sequential degradation, invalidated stale continuations, selected other admissible continuations and reached a typed terminal condition or principled refusal.
- **Causally operative morphology:** scoped interventions show that structural relations and acquired history matter to the implemented continuation mechanism; coupled Conduct/Experience Prints produced a tested joint phenotype absent under key controls.
- **Embedded feasibility:** STM32H723 laboratory decision traces were reproduced in scope; separate two-Print hardware evidence still requires independent raw-data adjudication.
- **Scientific discipline:** an equal-information conventional comparator matched certain bounded decisions. General performance superiority, field deployment and GNSS-denied waypoint arrival are **not established**.

These are **reported results within specified experiments**, not an end-to-end product certification. [TMC evidence and limits](docs/03_TMC_VISION.md) · [Public claims](evidence/CLAIMS_REGISTER.csv).

## Rights and collaboration status

**No product, commercial terms or transaction structure has been selected.** A potential system integrator may assess how TMC would complement an existing autopilot and other technologies; the ultimate customer, requirements, access model and any licensing or other arrangement remain undecided.

**KOHI BG EOOD** is the identified IP-holding entity, subject to documentary verification during any formal transaction. LMA and the other STASYS research directions are **not automatically included** in a TMC-specific discussion. This public repository provides claim-level summaries only: **no proprietary code, original raw evidence, canonical proof objects, implementation access or technology license**, including under NDA. Green CI checks documentation integrity only, not scientific validity, safety or patent clearance.
