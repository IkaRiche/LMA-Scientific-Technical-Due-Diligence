## 1. Distributed autonomy: what is actually demonstrated

The accepted digital relational line uses independently owned local organism states and a bounded peer-expression pathway. In the scoped two-organism experiment, a local need in one organism, another organism's own admitted observation and capability, an external authority/plant path, and subsequent observation of changed modeled surroundings close a causal loop.

The result is stronger than a visual animation: preregistered interventions that suppress peer expression, local observation, actuation or post-effect admission change the expected outcome; deterministic replay was recorded.

**Do not claim:** an absence of all centralized infrastructure. The simulation host owns the world clock, modeled physics and the production of local observations. These are distinct from a central *mission decision maker*, but they preclude the statement that every layer is decentralized or independent of a common environment.

**Reviewer's key discrimination:** separate autonomous decision ownership from a host that supplies the correct answer or selects agents behind the scenes. Ask which state is owned by each participant and which interventions demonstrate causal necessity.

## 2. Coordinate exchange and the meeting-point question are different propositions

**Proposition A — peer-message semantics:** in one frozen relational closure, the inter-organism semantic channel was limited to physiological LMIO. The test reports no peer-delivered coordinates, target vectors, assignment or result packets in the tested A/B path. This is **supported within that fixture**.

**Proposition B — local sensing:** each participant may still receive geometry-related information from its **own local observation**. The frozen simulation generates observations from modeled world state. Neither this source nor the public packet establishes raw-data-free perception.

**Proposition C — meeting without coordinate payloads:** an independent civilian synthetic study of local feedback and a moving service station showed limited meeting/co-location behavior without supplying coordinates through the receiver channel. Some controls failed or improved outcomes as expected; later mobility studies gave negative results for stronger hypotheses. This is not a native Guardian Veil result and not evidence of an operational interception or rendezvous system.

**Proposition D — general computation of a meeting point from LMIO alone:** **NOT ESTABLISHED**. An architectural description of locally derived meeting objectives is not a substitute for a qualified general mechanism. A shared simulation clock or host-generated local geometry must not be mistaken for a proved elimination of raw information.

The concise claim appropriate for external discussion is:

> We have bounded digital evidence for local functional coordination with no target-coordinate transfer in the specified peer channel; we do **not** yet claim a generally verified coordinate-free meeting-point calculator or an operational sensor-to-actuator system.

## 3. Terminal mission conduct: bounded proof versus product intent

TMC is defined as **active onboard mission conduct**, not passive risk monitoring or an ALLOW/BLOCK gate. A mission goal and permitted means come from outside; TMC's own computation forms subsequent mission intents in light of admitted current context and acquired history. Independent external components retain execution authority.

Accepted bounded software experiments include a single assigned goal continuing through successive degradation, rejection of a stale continuation, selection of another morphology-valid continuation, use of a valid remaining suffix, terminal completion, and a fail-closed refusal control. Coupled Conduct/Experience Prints demonstrate an additional **scoped** joint phenotype absent in isolated or uncoupled controls. A fair full-information conventional comparator can reproduce the tested output; no universal computational superiority is claimed.

A frozen STM32H723 platform-facing bench report records two identical decision traces and a measured replan-cycle observation in that scenario. The on-target result does **not** establish all-input WCET, readiness to fly, mission execution authority, or portability across hardware. Scientific adjudication of other original two-Print raw hardware evidence is separately pending.

## 4. Integrated capability boundary

The following implication is **invalid**:

`Veil digital PASS + separate civilian meeting PASS + TMC MCU PASS => verified integrated autonomous field system`.

No combined qualification is currently claimed. In particular, the public package does not assert reliable meeting/interception, performance under arbitrary signal loss, GNSS independence, fast-target protection, or autonomous physical mission completion.
