# 07 — Public and restricted disclosure boundary

**CURRENT STATUS: PUBLIC-SUMMARY ONLY.** No legal opinion or clearance for publication of additional patent-sensitive details is implied.

## Scope of the public package

- research purpose and high-level terminology;
- already-described bounded finite-model claims, **without** canonical proprietary proof/witness content;
- results at the level of scope, verification status and relevant evidence owners;
- review methodology, decision gates, limits and contact route.

## Intentionally excluded

- unreleased patent claims, figures, exact embodiments, engineering drawings, protected configurations and sensitive design dependencies;
- original TMC/DRC/Veil control source, binary firmware, special-purpose sensor or engagement algorithms;
- raw UART traces, privileged logs, source credentials, customer identities and unpublished source datasets;
- detailed parameters, performance envelopes or integration instructions for countering specific weapons or high-speed platforms;
- complete mathematical witness/proof objects requiring unpublished operator details.

## Non-disclosure policy for external review

The following materials remain internal to the rights holder and are **not provided** to external reviewers or investors, even under NDA: source code, repository access, canonical mathematical proofs and witnesses, raw experiment and hardware records, unpublished algorithms, operative morphology and implementation parameters.

External diligence may use written public summaries, conceptual discussions, non-sensitive high-level outputs, and owner-operated demonstrations where the rights holder retains custody and control of all underlying material.

No private evidence-room, read-only repository account, downloadable proprietary archive, source escrow, or reproduction package is offered.

## What a public repository cannot establish

External reviewers will not be able to verify all mathematical proofs or hardware evidence from this sanitized pack alone. That is a conscious limitation, **not** a claim of independent verification. If this disclosure boundary is insufficient for an external reviewer, the proper conclusion is that independent verification is unavailable; protected material will not be released.

## Legal note

A public GitHub repository is disclosure, even if its content has no open-source license. Patent applications do not automatically cover later unpublished improvements. Final publication requires review by the company and qualified patent counsel.
