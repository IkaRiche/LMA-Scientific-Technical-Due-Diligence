# 13 — Morphic AI: acquired functional Tissue and Morphic Memory

**Public evidence snapshot:** 3 November 2026  
**Purpose:** scientific and technical orientation for external reviewers. This is a curated summary of accepted, bounded results, not an inventory of internal research work or independent replication.

## The research proposition

Morphic AI investigates computation in which independently constituted, locally homeostatic **Lomidze Prints** acquire a **jointly operative Tissue** through their own admitted needs and lawful morphological development. The acquired morphology can retain causal significance for later native functions.

The principal result reported in the current source-native research is **a useful jointly acquired Tissue-level computational function**. In the tested bounded digital conditions, the jointly acquired shared structure performs a function that the independently operating constituent Prints and matched passive separations do not perform. This is stronger than observing mere contacts, message exchange or a post hoc record of an event.

The result is about **a particular causal organization of computation**, not a universal claim that a conventional algorithm cannot reproduce the same finite arithmetic.

## Precise LMA terms

- **Print:** independently constituted physiological entity with typed operative morphology, its own admitted observation, native homeostasis, lawful development and continuity.
- **Tissue:** the acquired jointly operative native morphology of independent Prints; not simply a roster, topology diagram or messaging network.
- **Morphic Memory:** the causal persistence of acquired operative morphology itself, not a separate cache, history database or scalar marker.
- **Chirp:** bounded physiological expression of need, not a command, assigned partner or answer-bearing message.

These conceptual descriptions are not a release of proprietary formal operators, source code or physical implementation parameters.

## Why the finding is technically significant

The key scientific distinction is between three different things:

1. **Independent functions in proximity.** Mere coexistence of functions is not Tissue under the LMA ontology.
2. **An acquired operative shared structure.** Native acquisition changes which useful joint operation is actually possible for continuing Prints.
3. **Retained acquired function.** The same source-native shared morphology remains causally operative on later, changed inputs without resetting the living constituents.

The reported experiments substantiate the second and third points in their **bounded digital setting**. A jointly acquired capability need not entail invention of a new elementary function opcode. It is the lawful shared operative composition, and its causal effect, that is under study.

Conventional finite-state or explicitly connected typed software can represent finite input/output behavior. No universal advantage in accuracy, performance, resources, complexity or representational irreducibility is claimed by these findings.
